## 🕹️ About

The Lost Wild is a first-person survival horror adventure from Great Ape Games and Annapurna Interactive. Stranded in a lush wilderness filled with dinosaurs, you must explore abandoned research facilities, gather useful items, and uncover the mystery at the heart of the island. Dinosaurs behave like wild animals, so survival depends on observation, stealth, distractions, environmental awareness, and carefully timed escapes. The game is planned for Windows PC and PlayStation 5 in 2027. Recent development work has focused on smoother performance, faster loading, and improved memory usage.

The Lost Wild is for players who enjoy atmospheric first-person horror, stealth, exploration, and tense encounters shaped by observation rather than firepower.

## ✨ Features

- 🌿 **Primal Wilderness** — Explore a cinematic island where dense vegetation hides danger and discovery.
- 📻 **Island Mystery** — Follow a mysterious radio signal while piecing together the story of the island.
- 🏚️ **Abandoned Facilities** — Search overgrown research sites for supplies, clues, and answers.
- 🥷 **Evasion-Based Survival** — Move quietly, create distractions, hide, and use the environment to escape.
- 🦖 **Living Predators** — Encounter dinosaurs with natural instincts, behaviors, and unpredictable reactions.
